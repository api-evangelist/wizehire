Your best candidates might already be one conversation away. When you ask your current employees to recommend people they know for open roles, you're tapping into something no job board can replicate: a personal endorsement from someone who already understands your culture, your expectations, and what it takes to succeed on your team. That's the core of an employee referral program, and the numbers make a strong case for formalizing it.
