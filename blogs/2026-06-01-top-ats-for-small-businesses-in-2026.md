Hiring is getting harder to predict. Candidates have more options, job boards are more crowded, and small business owners are spending more time sorting through applications than actually making hiring decisions. A good applicant tracking system (ATS) for small businesses brings structure to the process, cuts the manual busywork, and helps you focus on the right people faster.
