# WizeHire

> WizeHire is a hiring platform that helps small businesses find, assess, and hire great employees. The platform combines AI-powered candidate matching (Talent Match), an AI recruiting assistant (Scout), DISC+ personality assessments, expert hiring coaches, and a full-service applicant tracking system. WizeHire serves small and mid-size businesses across industries including real estate, insurance, dental, legal, automotive, and hospitality.
